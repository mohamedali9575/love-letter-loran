To My Beloved
Loran

From the very first moment our eyes met,
the universe whispered your name into my soul.

You are the poem I never knew I was writing,
the melody my heart had been humming
since before I knew how to speak.

In the garden of my life, you are the rarest bloom —
the rose that never fades, the star that never dims.
Every breath I take carries a piece of you,
every heartbeat echoes your name.

“I loved you yesterday, I love you still today,
and I will love you for all of eternity.”
Forever & Always Yours
